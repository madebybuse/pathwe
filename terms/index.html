Your story is yours

Everything you put into PathWe belongs to you. It stays on your iPhone, where nobody behind PathWe can read it, use it or pass it on.

Keep your own backup

PathWe has no cloud. If this iPhone is lost or reset, or the app is deleted, what is in it goes too. An encrypted iPhone backup — through iCloud or a computer — is the only thing standing between you and losing your story, so please keep one. Nothing can be recovered for you afterwards.

PathWe Plus

Plus unlocks the extra themes and app icons and lifts the limits on how much you can add. Monthly and yearly plans renew by themselves until you cancel; the lifetime plan is a single purchase. All of it is billed by the App Store — manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings, and ask Apple for a refund. Cancelling deletes nothing you have written; the Plus features simply lock again.

Fair use

PathWe is for your own memories. Please don't take it apart, resell it, or use it to hold someone else's information without their knowing.

What PathWe can and can't promise

PathWe is offered as it is. It is built carefully and tested, but no app can promise it will never carry a bug or lose data, and PathWe is not liable for what such a failure costs you. Where the law gives you rights as a consumer, nothing written here takes them away.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with them and the new version arrives with the next update.
